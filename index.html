About Let's Score

Let's Score is an educational platform dedicated to Science for Classes VI–X and Physics for Classes XI–XII. Our goal is to provide clear, concept-based and exam-oriented educational resources for Assamese and English medium students.

Explore NCERT-based learning resources, notes, MCQs, previous year question papers and test-oriented materials designed to make learning simpler and more effective.
